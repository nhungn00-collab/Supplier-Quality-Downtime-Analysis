# Supplier Quality & Downtime Analysis

This project analyzes supplier quality, production defects, and operational downtime to identify high-risk vendors and improve manufacturing performance.

## Overview
- Evaluated defect patterns across suppliers, plants, and material types
- Measured operational impact using downtime analysis
- Identified high-risk vendors contributing to production inefficiencies

## Objectives
- Detect suppliers with poor quality performance
- Evaluate relationship between defects and operational downtime
- Identify plants and materials with high defect concentration
- Support supplier management and operational improvement decisions

## Key Insights

### Vendor Risk
- Solholding recorded the highest defect count overall
- Vendor Plustax generated extremely high downtime despite lower defect volume
- This suggests that defect severity matters more than defect quantity alone

### Plant Analysis
- Defects are heavily concentrated in the Detroit, MI plant
- Supplier inconsistency and plant operating conditions may contribute to higher defect rates

### Material Quality
- Raw Materials show the highest concentration of quality issues
- Supplier quality directly impacts production efficiency and downtime

## Business Impact
The analysis shows that supplier quality significantly affects:
- Product defect rates
- Machine downtime
- Overall operational efficiency

High-risk suppliers require immediate evaluation, while stable suppliers can be considered strategic long-term partners.


## Recommendations

### Supplier Management
- Reevaluate vendors with high defect and downtime metrics
- Establish periodic supplier quality assessments
- Prioritize collaboration with high-performing suppliers

### Quality Control
- Strengthen incoming material inspections
- Focus on high-risk material categories

### Process Improvement
- Investigate root causes of recurring defects
- Collaborate with plants to optimize manufacturing processes

### Monitoring & Dashboard
Develop real-time dashboards for:
- Vendor defects
- Plant downtime
- Downtime trends over time
